02

An objective, with room to breathe

Display equations give the main idea its own space. Here, two losses are combined with a weighting coefficient.

\[ \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{NTP}}(\theta) + \lambda\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BEV}}(\theta) \]

03

A derivation, line by line

Aligned expressions keep each step of a calculation easy to follow.

\[ \begin{aligned} \theta^{\star} &= \arg\min_{\theta}\;\mathcal{L}(\theta) \\ \mathbf{g}_k &= \nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}(\theta_k) \\ \theta_{k+1} &= \theta_k - \eta\,\mathbf{g}_k \end{aligned} \]
LaTeX source 
\[
\begin{aligned}
\theta^{\star} &= \arg\min_{\theta}\;\mathcal{L}(\theta) \\
\mathbf{g}_k &= \nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}(\theta_k) \\
\theta_{k+1} &= \theta_k - \eta\,\mathbf{g}_k
\end{aligned}
\]

04

Vectors and matrices

A planar rotation is a compact example of matrix notation.

\[ \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf{x}'} = \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} \cos\phi & -\sin\phi \\ \sin\phi & \cos\phi \end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf{R}(\phi)} \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf{x}} \]
LaTeX source 
\[
\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf{x}'} =
\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix}
\cos\phi & -\sin\phi \\
\sin\phi & \cos\phi
\end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf{R}(\phi)}
\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}}_{\mathbf{x}}
\]

05

Probability and integrals

Fractions, integrals, and subscripts share the same typography as the simpler expressions above.

$$ \begin{aligned} p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D}) &= \frac{p(\mathcal{D} \mid \theta)\,p(\theta)}{p(\mathcal{D})} \\ p(\mathcal{D}) &= \int p(\mathcal{D} \mid \theta)\,p(\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta \end{aligned} $$
LaTeX source 
$$
\begin{aligned}
p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D})
&= \frac{p(\mathcal{D} \mid \theta)\,p(\theta)}{p(\mathcal{D})} \\
p(\mathcal{D})
&= \int p(\mathcal{D} \mid \theta)\,p(\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta
\end{aligned}
$$
