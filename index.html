Caio Azevedo

I am a second year PhD student at Mines Paris – PSL and Stellantis. My research focuses on trajectory prediction and vision-language-action models for autonomous driving.

Before my PhD I did internships at Huawei, the University of Osaka and Stellantis.

I completed my master's at Université Paris-Dauphine after the Cycle Ingénieur Polytechnicien of École Polytechnique, to where I transferred from the Insituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA) in Brazil.
